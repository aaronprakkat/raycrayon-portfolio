## Felines — design breakdown

Each Feline's detail board is built from six fields present for every piece, whether or not Ryan wrote a caption for it. This replaces an earlier spec that only had content for some pieces.

1. **Main render** — the artwork image itself.
2. **Palette** — swatches sampled from the artwork (build from the image, not from this doc).
3. **Style** — one line on what's visually distinct about this piece in the series.
4. **Font** — same on every board, not per-piece: bold condensed all-caps display type, stamped top and bottom, matching Ryan's own posts.
5. **Elements used** — literal shapes/objects visible in the render, written from actually looking at the image.
6. **Explanation** — Ryan's real caption where he wrote one; a plain visual observation where he didn't. Never invented intent. Credits fold in here (not a separate slot).

**Feline #1 — House Cat**
- Style: Warm pink-to-orange gradient ground; softer, rounder toy-blob forms than the later pieces.
- Elements used: inflated cream/peach claw-paw forms; cobalt triangular claw tips; one pink dagger-like spike; a concentric blue-and-purple dial resembling an iris; a small gold star-shaped cat head with ears on top.
- Explanation: No process caption beyond a credit to @azpeger.tat's cat photos as inspiration — visual description only, nothing invented.

**Feline #2 — Jaguar**
- Style: Cool icy blue, swirling vortex background, more mechanical/interlocking than organic.
- Elements used: pale blue glossy interlocked ring/donut forms; a hot-pink spiked chain wrapping through and around them; a paw-print detail.
- Explanation: "My laptop hasn't gotten a break from rendering." (real caption)

**Feline #3 — Cheetah**
- Style: Lime/citrus green, balloon-animal-like twisted tube forms, swirling vortex backdrop.
- Elements used: glossy lime balloon-twist tubes; small yellow star accents; green spiral background.
- Explanation: "Went for a Lemon flavour. I can taste the video." (real caption)

**Feline #4 — Lion**
- Style: Fire palette (orange/red/mustard), radial and explosive — the most aggressive piece in the series so far.
- Elements used: a large orange ring/plate; red conical spikes radiating outward like a mane or claws; a mustard-yellow star medallion; a flame pattern across the background.
- Explanation: "Ketchup and Mustard. I've been making too many food colour palettes I should finally eat." (real caption)

**Feline #5 — Lynx**
- Style: Hot pink with a cobalt wire structure woven through; the most abstract face treatment in the series by his own note.
- Elements used: glossy hot-pink paw/claw forms; a cobalt looping wire/vein structure threaded through them; a visible paw pad detail.
- Explanation: "I wanted to be more abstract with the face design, which is surprisingly the most satisfying part of the challenge." Credits @mehu1x's Adobe subscription as making the piece possible.

**Feline #6 — Ocelot**
- Style, Elements used: **unknown — the finished poster artwork has never been seen.** Only the process breakdown image exists (three stages: Solid Viewport → Eevee → Cycles+Lighting). Do not write style/elements copy until the final artwork is obtained from Ryan.
- Explanation: none available yet.

**Feline #7 — Panther**
- Style: Lavender/purple, the most "mecha" piece in the series — concentric mechanical dial forms rather than soft blobs.
- Elements used: lavender glossy forms; pink cat-ear/bow shapes at the top; a large concentric dial with a starburst center and a ring of triangular spikes.
- Explanation: "Took more time into polishing the details... a bit of a mecha/stand combo with this piece. At the end of the day I wanna see my whole collection in One Piece." (real caption — also usable as a closing line for the Felines section, since it's his own stated ambition for the set)
